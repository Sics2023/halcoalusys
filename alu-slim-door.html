ALU Slim Door

Alu Slim Doors combine minimalist aesthetics with strength, durability, and smooth functionality. Designed with slimline aluminium profiles and larger glass panels, these doors maximise natural light while creating a clean, contemporary architectural appearance.

Engineered for modern residential and commercial spaces, they offer excellent strength-to-weight performance, corrosion resistance, low maintenance, and long-lasting durability. The slim frame design provides wider uninterrupted views and seamlessly connects indoor and outdoor spaces.
