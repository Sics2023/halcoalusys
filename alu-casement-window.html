ALU Casement Window

The HALCO ALU Casement Window is a refined aluminium casement window system with a smooth, curved-edge profile, designed to bring elegance and functionality together. With support for heights up to 1750mm and shutter widths up to 800mm, it offers superior performance in air tightness, insulation, and wind resistance—making it ideal for upscale living and hospitality environments.
