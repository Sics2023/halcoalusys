ALU Elliptical Louver

The HALCO ALU Elliptical Louver and Baffle Ceiling Systems offer the perfect synergy of design, functionality, and durability for both interior and exterior applications. Available in vertical and horizontal configurations, these systems provide sun shading, privacy, and façade articulation without compromising on aesthetics.

Crafted from high-grade aluminium, they are available in a wide range of powder-coated and wood grain finishes, delivering the natural warmth of wood and the performance of aluminium—making them ideal for facades, balconies, courtyards, ceilings, and bespoke feature elements.
