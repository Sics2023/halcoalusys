With over a decade of industry leadership and a dedicated team of 400+ professionals, HALCO proudly stands as Eastern India’s largest integrated manufacturer of architectural aluminium systems.

Our 150,000 sq. ft. state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities operate under rigorous quality controls, transforming 99.7% pure primary aluminium ingots into precision-engineered end products. From robust architectural window systems to refined interior solutions, our products embody durability, design, and performance. Through our specialized division, ALUSYS, we deliver unique and efficient fenestration and interior solutions that elevate modern living and working spaces. Every system we craft reflects our commitment to functional elegance and architectural integrity.

Our Mission

Engineering aluminium solutions that shape modern spaces with innovation, precision, and integrity

Our Vision

To be a global leader in sustainable architectural systems, setting the standard for quality, design, and innovation in aluminium manufacturing
