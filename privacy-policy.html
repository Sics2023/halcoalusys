Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HALCO Alusys (“HALCO”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects the information you share with us when you use this website. By using the website you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us:

  • Brochure requests – your name and phone number when you download our brochure.
  • Enquiries – your name, phone number, email address and message when you use the contact form.
  • Newsletter – your email address if you subscribe to updates.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical data such as your browser type, device and the pages you visit. We do not use this to identify you.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and send quotations or product information you request.
  • To follow up on brochure requests and help you choose the right aluminium system.
  • To send news and product updates, if you have subscribed.
  • To improve our website, products and services.

3. How your information is shared

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Form submissions are delivered to our team by email through a third-party form-processing service, which handles the data only to deliver it to us. We may share details with our authorised dealers or installation partners only when needed to serve your enquiry, or when required by law.

4. Cookies

This website uses only essential browser storage, for example to remember that you have already requested the brochure. We do not use advertising cookies.

5. Data retention and security

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law, and take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates. Write to us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date it was last updated.
