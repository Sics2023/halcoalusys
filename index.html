About Company

At Halco
innovation and excellence are at the heart of everything we do.

With over a decade of industry leadership and a dedicated team of 400+ professionals, HALCO proudly stands as Eastern India’s largest integrated manufacturer of architectural aluminium systems.

Our 150,000 sq. ft. state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities operate under rigorous quality controls, transforming 99.7% pure primary aluminium ingots into precision-engineered end products. From robust architectural window systems to refined interior solutions, our products embody durability, design, and performance.

Through our specialized division, ALUSYS, we deliver unique and efficient fenestration and interior solutions that elevate modern living and working spaces. Every system we craft reflects our commitment to functional elegance and architectural integrity.

Why Aluminium ?
01

Longer Lifespan

Durability is an important advantage of aluminium. It is a particularly robust material that stands out from the rest, such as PVC, as it gives frames a longer lifespan. Thus, unlike other materials, aluminium frames are not at risk of deterioration, making them a value for money choice in the long term, reaching even over three decades.

02

Consistent Performance in the Long Term

Modern aluminium windows ensure stability and offer top performance in the field of thermal insulation, waterproofing, air permeability, and sound insulation, overall enhancing a space and contributing to the improvement of the living quality.

03

Variety of Construction & Design Solutions

When it comes to choosing new aluminum windows, you have numerous options. In terms of color choices, the variety offered by aluminum windows is unmatched by wood or PVC frames. Surface treatment options for aluminium windows and doors include: Powder Coating, Anodizing, Metallic Color and Bicolor (different color of the external/internal profile).

04

Energy Savings

By using aluminium energy-efficient frames, you can reduce your monthly household expenses during cold and warm weather.

05

Added Value to Your Property

Your frames are the main "line of defense" for your place. By choosing ALUSYS aluminium energy frames, you not only strengthen your home but also significantly increase its value!

06

Slim and Strong

System aluminium windows are made with profiles which have the highest strength to weight ratio and is a sleek yet robust material unlike PVC which needs concealed steel reinforcement and are bulky. Even with the reinforcement, PVC windows have a very limited window height capacity due to limitations of reinforcement.
