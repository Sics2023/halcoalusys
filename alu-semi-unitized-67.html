ALU SEMI-UNITIZED 67

The HALCO ALU Semi-Unitized 67 System is engineered for modern architectural façades requiring a balance of performance, aesthetics, and efficiency. Designed to accommodate both single and double glazed units (SGU & DGU), these systems offer structural integrity with minimal visible aluminium and optimal thermal and acoustic performance.

Ideal for commercial buildings, high-rises, hotels, malls, and institutional projects, these systems combine the speed of unitized installation with the flexibility of stick systems, making them a cost-effective and scalable solution for complex façades.
