ALU Casement Smart Window

The HALCO ALU Casement Smart Window is an economical and reliable casement window system built for everyday residential use. Supporting heights up to 1500mm and shutter widths up to 600mm, it is compatible with DGU and laminated glass and delivers dependable air tightness, wind load resistance and thermal insulation for bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens and utility spaces.
